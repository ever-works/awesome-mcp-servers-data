## Features

- Real-time macro and market headlines from central banks, statistics agencies and financial media, merged so one story is one event
- Economic calendar for the week with forecast, previous and actual values
- Primary-source releases from the Fed, ECB, Bank of England, Bank of Japan, BLS, BEA and SEC
- 7-day searchable archive and a one-call briefing for a single asset (EUR/USD, gold, oil, BTC, indices)

## Use Cases

- "What moved EUR/USD in the last hour?"
- "What high-impact USD events are on the calendar this week?"
- "What did the Fed publish this week?"
