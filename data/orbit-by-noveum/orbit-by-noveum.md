## Overview

Orbit by Noveum provides task management for people and AI agents, including issues, projects, sprints, docs and files.

## Connection

Connect to `https://orbit.noveum.ai/mcp` using Streamable HTTP. Sign in to a free Orbit account and approve workspace-scoped OAuth permissions. No API key is required.

[Connection and permissions guide](https://github.com/Noveum/orbit/blob/main/docs/mcp.md).

## Source and self-hosting

[Apache-2.0 source](https://github.com/Noveum/orbit) contains the full application, including the MCP implementation. See the repository for current deployment requirements.

## Pricing

The hosted service is free. Self-hosting requires your own infrastructure.
